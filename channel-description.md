# STICKOCALYPSE — Channel Description

Day 47. The city fell in one night.

Welcome to STICKOCALYPSE — THE LAST SURVIVORS. A cinematic stickman apocalypse series in 60-second episodes, made for YouTube Shorts.

Alex, Max, Luna and Shadow are fighting to survive in a burning city overrun by the horde. Every episode is a new piece of the story: a locked bunker door, a radio signal that should not exist, a safe zone that might be a lie — and a trap already set.

Meet the survivors:
- Alex — the leader. Red bandana, glowing baton, never backs down.
- Max — the defender. Loyal, strong, first into danger.
- Luna — the medic. Calm under fire, and the reason they are still alive.
- Shadow — the mystery. Hooded, silent, watching. Friend… or trap?

New episodes continue the story — enter the bunker, follow the tunnel, and find out who is really sending the signal.

Subscribe and turn on notifications so you never miss an episode. The horde is coming. Will you survive?
