# STICKOCALYPSE — Episode 2: "THE DOOR OPENS"
## Storyboard (8 scenes x 7.5 s, 1080x1920, 30 FPS)

Style lock (every scene): cinematic 3D stickman animation. Alex: round black head, white expressive eyes, red bandana, dark tactical outfit, glowing orange baton. Max: round dark-charcoal head, white eyes, broad shoulders, dark green tactical vest. Shadow: dark black silhouette, hooded clothing, glowing eyes, lightweight equipment. Luna: round black head WITH LONG DARK HAIR TIED IN A PONYTAIL, expressive eyes, dark survival jacket, medical backpack with a white cross, NEVER wears a red bandana (the bandana is Alex's only). Bunker interior: cold blue-grey concrete, flickering lights, old radio equipment, shelves of supplies. Same burning-city night look outside as Episode 1 v5. STRICT ANATOMICAL CONSISTENCY: the supplied reference image is the absolute source of truth — same head size/shape, shoulder width, torso/arm/leg length, limb thickness, eye placement, silhouette, same body model throughout. ROUND spherical heads, NOT square, NOT blocky, NOT cube-shaped. Never redesign the characters.
